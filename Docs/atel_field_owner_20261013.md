The retained complete decompile archive has three direct controller constructor routes. `00875490` constructs id0 with one primary script; its known callers are the field loader `00872e90` and wrapper `00875460`. Its only direct caller in the retained archive is `007983A0`; its three selections pass ids2,3,4, with character/monster/battle-message setup callers `007830D0`, `00784120`, `00797560`. Segmented global worker indices and per-script local indices differ, so field offsets and parent-file bounds must not be silently applied to these controllers. The third constructor `00875930` has the retained caller `008BE370`, which passes id6 and embedded static data.

Their existing exact physical EV allocation receipt, logical file length, chunk bounds, serial, native header, generation/resource retirement and prepared-copy checks remain. A future feature consuming ids2/3/4/6 requires its own proven physical extent/lifetime and local/global semantics; no current field producer is added there.
